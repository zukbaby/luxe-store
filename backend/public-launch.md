# LUXE STORE public link and domain plan

## Current public production link

Use this project-level production domain for the public storefront:

https://luxe-store-zukbaby.vercel.app/

This is a project domain, not a per-deployment preview URL. New successful production deployments on the connected main branch are served on the project domain.

## Long-term brand domain

A custom domain registered and controlled by the LUXE STORE owner is the preferred long-term public identity (for example, a chosen LUXE STORE .com domain, subject to availability). A domain registration is renewable, not literally permanent; keep its registration and DNS settings active.

Do not buy, transfer, or change a domain without the owner's explicit approval. Do not publish an unowned or unverified domain.

## Deployment and visitor experience

- Share the project-level production URL, not an individual deployment URL or Vercel dashboard URL.
- Visitors should land on the storefront root.
- Vercel remains the hosting provider behind the website.
- Keep preview/deployment access protections in place; do not make private previews public just to share the storefront.
- When a custom domain is approved and connected, update canonical and social-share metadata to that domain and verify HTTPS and redirects.

## Launch truth

The current storefront remains a prototype. Cart state is stored locally; real accounts, database-backed products, checkout, payments, seller payouts, verification approvals, DAMA AI and Vicky AI are not live until their secure backend integrations are completed and tested.
