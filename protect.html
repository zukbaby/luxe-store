LUXE PROTECT

A clearer, safer marketplace experience built around transparent listings, seller accountability and support when something goes wrong.

Know who you're buying from

Product pages are designed to show the seller, product details and marketplace status clearly. Seller fulfilment remains the seller's responsibility.

Report a concern

The planned report flow will let customers flag suspicious sellers, misleading listings, suspected scams, unsafe content and intellectual-property concerns for human review.

Get support

DAMA can guide customers to the right support channel. Payment disputes, order issues and account concerns must be escalated to a human reviewer.

Important: LUXE PROTECT is a feature concept, not an active guarantee, insurance product or payment protection scheme. Reporting, dispute handling, refund rules and seller enforcement must be implemented and published before launch. LUXE STORE is a marketplace and does not operate sellers' warehouses or delivery services.
