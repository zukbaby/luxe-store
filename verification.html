Verified Creator

LUXE BADGE EXPLANATION · PREVIEW

This badge is intended to show that LUXE STORE has reviewed a creator account against its published creator-verification criteria and confirmed that the account meets those requirements.

It helps visitors recognise an account whose creator identity has completed the LUXE review process. It does not mean LUXE endorses every post, product, opinion or transaction from that creator.

How verification works: applications are checked against clear criteria, Vicky AI may help prepare a neutral review summary, and an authorised human reviewer makes the final decision.

Important: the badge shown on this page is a design preview. LUXE STORE is not currently issuing real verification badges.
